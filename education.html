Education & Youth

Our education work creates access to learning for children and young people across Cuttack district: the annual felicitation of the district's best Matriculation students, school art competitions, and the Odisha Yuva Sansad youth parliament that puts young people behind a microphone.

Each year the Foundation felicitates the top ten and every A1-grade student in the Cuttack district Matriculation examinations. Students come to a packed auditorium with their parents and teachers, and receive a trophy and a certificate from the hands of guests on stage.

The ceremony is deliberately formal, a lamp lighting, an Odissi performance, dignitaries in attendance, a full house. For many of the students it is the first time their work has been recognised outside their own school.

In February 2026 the Foundation also ran an art competition, 'Our Flag, Our Story', celebrating Odisha culture. Open to classes five to ten at Cambridge School, Barabati Stadium, with prizes of ₹5,000, ₹3,000 and ₹2,000.

Questions

Frequently asked

How does the Foundation support education?

Through the annual Sambardhana Utchab felicitation of the district's best Matriculation students, school art competitions, and the Odisha Yuva Sansad youth parliament.

Who can take part in the art competition?

Students of classes five to ten. Entry is free and participants bring their own colours and brushes.

What is the Odisha Yuva Sansad?

A youth parliament that gives delegates a seat, a nameplate and a microphone, and asks them to debate the questions the state actually has to answer. It is free to enter.
