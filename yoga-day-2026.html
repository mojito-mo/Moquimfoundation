International Yoga Day 2026

The Foundation laid mats across an entire ground and filled them, one of the district's largest public wellness gatherings.

On the morning of 21 June 2026 the Foundation laid out mats across an entire ground in Cuttack and filled them. Schoolchildren, families, doctors, officials and volunteers came together for one of the district's largest public wellness gatherings.

The programme opened with the lighting of the ceremonial lamp, followed by a guided session led from the main stage. Odia television crews covered the event from the ground. The whole gathering was free and open to anyone who came.

The event was organised end to end by the Foundation and its volunteers, from the venue branding and direction hoardings to the invitation cards, mats and refreshments.
