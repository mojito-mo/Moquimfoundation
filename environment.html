Environment

Every tree planted is a promise to the future. Through our Environmental Awareness Programs, Moquim Foundation works to inspire communities to take meaningful action towards environmental conservation and sustainable development. We believe protecting nature is essential to creating healthier communities and a safer future for generations to come.

Our Tree Plantation Programs and Green Initiatives encourage individuals and communities to participate in environmental protection, climate action and eco-friendly practices. From creating awareness to supporting community-led environmental activities, our work promotes responsible choices that can make a lasting difference.

We also recognise the importance of biodiversity protection and ecosystem restoration in maintaining healthy natural environments. Through community participation and environmental awareness, we encourage people to become active stewards of their surroundings.

True environmental change begins at the community level. By promoting community sustainability, environmental conservation and green initiatives, we are working towards a future where people and nature can thrive together.

Questions

Frequently asked

What environmental work does the Foundation do?

Tree plantation drives, environmental awareness programmes and community-led green initiatives, most recently on World Environment Day at Satyanagar Road, Cuttack.

Can my neighbourhood host a plantation drive?

Yes. The Foundation provides saplings, tools and organisation. Residents doing the planting themselves is the point, because a sapling your neighbour planted gets watered again next week.

How many saplings have been planted?

Around two hundred at the 2026 World Environment Day drive. That figure is our own estimate and has not been independently verified.
