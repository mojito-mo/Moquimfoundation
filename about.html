About Us

The Story Behind the Mission

Every individual deserves dignity, opportunity and the chance to live a better life. That belief sits at the heart of our work as a social welfare organisation and NGO in Odisha.

Our Social Welfare Journey

A social welfare organisation and NGO in Cuttack creating community impact

Every meaningful movement begins with a belief. For Moquim Foundation, that belief is simple: every individual deserves dignity, opportunity and the chance to live a better life.

The Foundation’s journey was inspired by Dr. Mohammed Nayeem, whose dedication to serving underprivileged communities instilled in his son, Mohd. Moquim, a lasting commitment to community service, public welfare and social responsibility. That legacy became the inspiration behind building an organisation dedicated to creating meaningful and lasting community impact.

Today, Moquim Foundation is a social welfare organization and non profit organization in Odisha, working as a community development non-profit organization and charity organization focused on strengthening underserved communities. Registered at Purighat, Ring Road, Cuttack, the Foundation brings together community welfare, public welfare and sustainable social development through meaningful initiatives, and its work reaches Bhubaneswar as well, where the Odisha Yuva Sansad was held.

A Purpose to Serve. A Commitment to Change.

The work began in 2018, when Mohd. Moquim set out with a simple yet powerful purpose: to stand beside people and communities facing difficult circumstances and help create a life of greater dignity, opportunity and hope. The trust was formally registered on 7 January 2020 before the District Sub-Registrar, Cuttack. What began as a commitment to community service, public welfare and social responsibility has grown into a meaningful journey of creating lasting community impact.

Today, Moquim Foundation works as a community development NGO and charity organization across key areas of social development. From healthcare and education to women empowerment, food support and rural development, our work is focused on addressing essential needs and strengthening underserved communities. Rooted in Cuttack and reaching into Bhubaneswar, we continue to build a more inclusive and compassionate society through purposeful action and sustainable community welfare.

What Drives Us

Our vision, mission and values

Our Mission

To serve as a community development organisation in Odisha committed to uplifting marginalised and underserved communities through community service, public welfare and meaningful social action. Through healthcare, education, livelihood support, women empowerment and rural development, we work to help individuals and families live with greater dignity and opportunity.

Our Vision

To build an inclusive Odisha where every individual, whatever their background, has access to essential healthcare, education, opportunity and support. We envision stronger communities where dignity, equality and sustainable development form the foundation for a better future.

Compassion in Action

We approach every initiative with empathy and genuine care for the people we serve.

Dignity for All

Every individual deserves respect and the opportunity to live with dignity.

Social Responsibility

Public welfare is a shared responsibility, and we contribute where our efforts can matter.

Transparency & Trust

We publish what we do, with dates, venues and photographs, so anyone can check it.

Sustainable Development

We look past immediate relief towards change that lasts.

Community First

Every initiative begins by understanding the needs and realities of the people at its heart.

Vision & Mission

Our vision

An Odisha where no family is denied healthcare, food, education or dignity because of where they were born.

Our mission

To deliver accessible health, relief, education, sports and environmental programmes to underserved communities in Cuttack district and beyond, transparently, measurably, and in partnership with the people we serve.

Our values

  • Humanity first. Service without conditions, and without asking who someone is before helping them.
  • Transparency. Dates, places, photographs and honest numbers.
  • Locality. Led by the community it serves, in the district it belongs to.
  • Consistency. Programmes that return, not one-off gestures.
Humanity is our Priority.
